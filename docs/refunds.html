Refund Policy
for the NavMap unlock

Last updated: November 2026

This policy covers the one-time payment that unlocks NavMap on your Garmin watch. It does not cover anything you buy from Google, and it does not limit any rights you have under consumer law where you live.

1. What you are buying

One payment of ₹199 (or the local equivalent shown at checkout) unlocks NavMap for the Garmin account you bought it with. There is no subscription and nothing renews. The payment is taken by us through Razorpay, not by Garmin, so Garmin's store support cannot refund it — please come to us first.

2. You get a free trial first

NavMap runs for seven days before any payment is due, so you can confirm it works with your watch, your phone and your routes. Because of that, we ask that you request a refund only when something is genuinely wrong rather than because you changed your mind after using it for a while.

3. When we refund

  • Charged twice, or charged and not unlocked. Full refund, no questions asked. This is the case we most want to hear about.
  • The app does not work on a supported watch and we cannot fix it for you. Full refund within 30 days of purchase.
  • Bought by mistake. Full refund within 14 days of purchase, provided the unlock was not used for a long period.
  • We stop supporting your watch model. Prorated refund is not offered, but talk to us — we would rather be fair than be right.

4. When we cannot

  • Your watch is not a supported model. NavMap needs a circular AMOLED display and Connect IQ 5.0 or newer. Memory-in-Pixel watches such as the Fenix 7, Forerunner 255/955 and Instinct cannot run it. The supported list is on the home page and in the store listing.
  • Google Maps Platform charges on your own Google Cloud account. Map pictures and route lines use your own key and are billed to you by Google. We cannot refund Google's charges.
  • Google changes Google Maps so its navigation can no longer be read. We will try to fix it; if we cannot, a refund is reasonable and we will offer one for affected recent purchases.
  • You simply no longer want it long after buying, having used it for months.

5. How to ask

Email support@navmap.download with:

  • the Razorpay payment id or the date you paid,
  • the email or phone number you paid with,
  • your watch model, and
  • what went wrong.

We reply within three working days. If we agree, the refund goes back to the original payment method through Razorpay and usually lands within 5–10 working days, depending on your bank.

6. What a refund does not do

The unlock is held by Garmin against your account, and a refund does not remove it automatically — there is no switch on our side that revokes it. So a refund is given in good faith, and we ask you not to keep using the app after asking for one. Repeated requests of that kind may lead us to decline future ones.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. The policy that applied is the one published on the day you bought the unlock.
